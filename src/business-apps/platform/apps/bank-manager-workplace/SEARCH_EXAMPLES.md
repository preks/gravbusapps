## 💡 **How the AI Understanding Works:**

### **Synonyms Recognized:**
- "Kredit" = "Darlehen" = "Loan" = "Kreditantrag"
- "Urlaub" = "Vacation" = "Abwesenheit" = "Absence"
- "Kunden" = "Customers" = "Clients" = "Mandanten"
- "Team" = "Mitarbeiter" = "Employees" = "Personal"

### **Partial Matching:**
- "Kredit" finds "Kreditanträge prüfen"
- "Team" finds "Team-Übersicht", "Team Performance"
- "Genehm" finds "Genehmigungen"

### **Context-Aware:**
- Understands German AND English simultaneously
- Groups results by category (AI Suggestions, Actions, Navigation)
- Shows most relevant results first
- Confidence scores on AI suggestions
